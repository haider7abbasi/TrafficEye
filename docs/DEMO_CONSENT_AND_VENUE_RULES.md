# TrafficEye — Demo consent & venue rules (working draft)

**Purpose:** FYP / internal demo only. Replace or extend with your institution’s ethics form and supervisor guidance when required.

## Venue & filming

1. **Prefer controlled venues** for screen recording and photos: lab, parking lot with permission, or private property where you have explicit access. Avoid filming strangers’ faces or identifiable minors as the main subject.
2. **Public roads:** If you must demo on public roads, keep the **device mounted or hands-free** per local traffic law; do not operate the app while driving. A passenger or stationary setup is safer for video.
3. **License plates & vehicles:** Use **staged vehicles**, **consenting participants**, or **obscured/blurred plates** in published materials unless you have written consent. Do not ship real plate text or faces into the repo as “test fixtures” without clearance.

## Consent & disclosure

4. **Anyone on camera** in thesis slides, viva recordings, or public repos should know they are being recorded and how the footage will be used (coursework only, retention window, who can access it).
5. **Officer / tester accounts** in Firebase are test identities only; use synthetic emails where possible and do not use production police credentials.

## Data & retention

6. **Test media** stays local or in a **dedicated Firebase project** for development; delete demo violations after the course if they contain sensitive scenes.
7. **Roboflow / APIs:** Demo images sent to inference are subject to Roboflow’s terms; do not upload sensitive personal documents or illegal content.

## Document control

8. **Supervisor sign-off:** When your program requires it, attach this file (or a formal ethics appendix) to your FYP submission and obtain approval before filming at sensitive locations.
